# Terms and operating boundary

DPC-AIO Companion is an engineering workflow aid. Installing or invoking the plugin does not grant Android Device Owner, Profile Owner, Knox, root, system, or other privileged capabilities.

All Android management operations remain subject to the permissions, enrollment state, platform APIs, device policy, signing configuration, and authorization of the DPC-AIO application and target device. The companion must not represent mock or laboratory state as a genuine vendor license or credential.
